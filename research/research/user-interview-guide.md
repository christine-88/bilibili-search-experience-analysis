# Bilibili Learning Search — User Interview Guide

## 1. Research Objective

Understand how users choose learning videos on Bilibili, what information they rely on, and where they experience difficulty when evaluating search results.

## 2. Target Participants

- 3–5 people who have searched for educational videos on Bilibili
- Preferably include beginners who have recently learned a new skill

## 3. Interview Questions

1. Can you recall the last time you searched for a tutorial on Bilibili? What were you trying to learn?
2. What keywords did you use?
3. How did you decide which video to watch?
4. What information did you check before clicking a video?
5. Did you open several videos before choosing one? Why?
6. Was there any information you wanted but could not easily find?
7. What would make it easier for you to choose a suitable tutorial?

## 5. Research Notes and Limitations

Record participants' actual experiences without leading their answers. Separate direct statements from the researcher's interpretations. Findings from a small number of interviews are exploratory and should not be treated as representative of all Bilibili users.
